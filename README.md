# Insurance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
The **Insurance Analytics Dashboard** project focuses on transforming raw insurance data into meaningful business insights using data analytics and visualization techniques. This project helps analyze insurance policy performance, revenue trends, and operational efficiency through interactive dashboards and KPI tracking.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze insurance policy performance and business trends
- Generate actionable insights from insurance datasets
- Monitor key insurance KPIs
- Support business decision-making through data visualization

---

## Key Features
- Interactive dashboards for insurance analytics
- Policy performance tracking
- Revenue analysis and executive performance monitoring
- KPI-based reporting system
- Business intelligence insights for insurance operations

---

## KPIs Tracked
- Claim Ratio %
- Total Policies
- Total Active Policies
- Total Inactive Policies
- Policy Count by Group
- Stage-wise Revenue Analysis
- Revenue Collection by Executive
- Meeting & Revenue Performance Tracking

---

## Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and visualization
- **Tableau** – Data visualization and reporting
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and analysis
- **MySQL** – Data extraction and query analysis

---

## Project Workflow
1. Data Collection
2. Data Cleaning & Transformation
3. SQL Querying for KPI Extraction
4. Dashboard Development
5. Data Visualization & Reporting
6. Business Insight Generation

---

## Insights Generated
- Identified policy performance trends
- Compared active and inactive policy distribution
- Analyzed claim ratios for insurance performance evaluation
- Tracked revenue contribution by executives
- Monitored stage-wise business revenue growth
